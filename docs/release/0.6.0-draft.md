# 0.6.0 release notes: a draft

**Not released.** A draft for the owner to edit, from `CHANGELOG.md`'s *Unreleased* section. Tried on the owner's library
on 2026-11-06, except where *Before this ships* says otherwise. The version number is a suggestion.

## When something goes wrong

- ***Find the problem mod*** turns off half your mods, you play and say what happened, and in a few rounds one is left. Everything goes back at the end.
- ***What changed*** shows what's new since your last game with other mods, with *Go back to those mods*.
- **Shared files say what matters,** such as "41 could break quests, 43 only change how things look", most serious first.
- **A batch goes on without what fails,** and says which and why.
- **A mod inside a bigger one says so** and offers to keep the bigger one.
- **Home says when your mods can't reach the game,** because your profile names a documents folder the game doesn't read, and names both folders.
- **Removed copies go to the Recycle Bin,** so a mistake can be put back.
- **Backups stop filling your drive.** Files your Library already has aren't copied again, and *Undo* brings them back from there. Backups you already have keep the space they use.

## Fewer copies

- Adding a mod looks first. If it looks like something in your game, you're offered *Manage it*, so you take over what you have instead of adding a second copy.
- *Keep this one* removes a mod's other copies from your Library and gets the space back.
- *Keep one of each* does that for every mod you have more than once, from one preview, and it's offered as your next step. Bits of a mod left behind by moving from Vortex count too. Copies that really aren't the same come first and stay unless you pick one.

## A clearer path through your mods

DAORework now tells you what to do next, and stops you ending up with two copies of a mod.

- **One next step** on Home and the Mods page, worked out from where you are.
- **First steps** for a new player: find your game, add mods, turn them on.
- **Turning a mod on takes over what's already in your game.** Matching files are moved, not copied.
- **Take them over:** mods already in your game, in one preview, with *Who wins* and *Swap* where they really differ.
- ***Manage* clears leftovers** for mods already on, with a backup.

## Easier with a lot of mods

- One-line rows that open in place, with search and sort.
- *To top*, *To bottom* and drag in *Arrange*.
- Rows say which mods win the files they share.
- Previews stay in view, with *Apply* always showing, and a preview only warns about what matters.
- Big changes show their progress, and the window waits until they finish.
- Copies of a mod are noticed, and *Select all* picks one.
